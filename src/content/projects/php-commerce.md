Normalised schema across products, users, carts and orders, session-based
authentication, a checkout flow that keeps order integrity across multiple
tables, and a separate admin area for catalogue and order management.

Writing the queries by hand is the point — transaction boundaries and join
behaviour are things you feel here rather than read about.
